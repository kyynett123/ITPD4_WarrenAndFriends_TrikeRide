Official TODA Fare Assumptions & Formulas
📌 1. Flat Base Fare (First 1 – 2 Kilometers)

• ₱10.00 base fare applies to all trips up to 2.0 km within the same barangay or corridor (e.g. WIT to Luna St, CPU to Lopez Jaena).

📌 2. Distance Increment Surcharge (Per-KM Brackets)

• 2.1 to 3.0 km: +₱5.00 surcharge ➔ ₱15.00 (e.g. WIT to CPU or SM City)
• 3.1 to 5.0 km: +₱10.00 surcharge ➔ ₱25.00 (e.g. CPU to Tagbak Terminal)
• 5.1 to 8.0 km: +₱5.00 surcharge ➔ ₱30.00 (e.g. Jaro Plaza to Pavia Town Plaza)

📌 3. Special (Direct / Solo Hire) Multiplier Rule

• Special trips occupy the entire tricycle exclusively (3 passenger seats reserved) without waiting for others. Computed at a 3x multiplier: ₱30.00 base, ₱45.00 mid, ₱65.00 extended, and ₱80.00 outer.

📌 4. Statutory 20% Discount Provision

• Pursuant to national transport guidelines, Students, Senior Citizens, and PWDs enjoy a 20% discount on regular trips upon presenting a valid ID (₱10 becomes ₱8, ₱15 becomes ₱12, ₱25 becomes ₱20, ₱30 becomes ₱24).
